Added variance and standard error estimation from replicate weights for scalar statistics, preserving input dtypes and supporting common-factor jackknife, BRR, Fay's BRR, bootstrap and successive-difference schemes with explicit full-sample or replicate-mean centering. Reference-relative centering and scaled accumulation preserve representable variances at extreme magnitudes. Statistical validity depends on the statistic and survey design; nonsmooth quantiles can require an appropriate replication method or smoothing.
